What building with us actually means.

The full process

  1. You tell us what you need — the box above, or message us directly. This helps us build a preview that actually fits your business, instead of a generic template.
  2. Free preview — we build a working version of your site. No cost, no obligation. You judge the real thing, not a promise.
  3. You review & approve — two rounds of revisions included. Nothing goes live until you're happy, because you should never pay for something you haven't seen.
  4. Choose who sets up your accounts — for hosting, payments, and image storage, you can either create the accounts yourself and share access with us, or we create them using your business email and hand over full ownership once we're done. Either way, everything ends up under your name — this protects you if we ever stop working together.
  5. Payments, if you need them — you create your own Paystack account and complete verification yourself. This matters because Paystack pays out directly to the verified account holder — it has to be you either way for the money to reach your bank. We only ever need your Public and Secret API keys afterward, nothing more.
  6. We launch it — hosted under your own account, on your own domain. Fully yours, so no one can hold your business hostage later.
  7. Maintenance — entirely your choice — ongoing care (hosting monitoring, updates, support) is optional, not required. If you want it, we agree on it together; if not, the site is still fully yours and functional without it.
  8. Domain name — a custom web address (like yourbusiness.com) has a small yearly cost, paid by you directly to the registrar, not to us — this keeps the domain permanently in your name.
  9. Cost — pricing depends on scope and is discussed directly with you after you've seen the preview — it's negotiable, not fixed, because every business's needs are different.
  10. Final agreement — once you've approved the preview and we've talked through everything above, we confirm together on WhatsApp before any final build work begins.

What we'll need from you, and why

  • Brand colours or logo — so the site looks like your business, not a stranger's template.
  • Photos and your price list or menu — this is the actual content customers will see and act on.
  • How you'd like customers to reach you — WhatsApp, chat, or both — so the contact flow matches how you actually run your business.
  • Your decision on accounts and maintenance — covered above — so we build the right setup from day one instead of redoing it later.

🔒 We never ask for your BVN, NIN, passwords, or bank login — for anything, at any stage. If anyone claiming to be us ever asks for these, it isn't us.
